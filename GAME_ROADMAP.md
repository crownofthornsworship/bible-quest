# Bible Quest — Improvement Roadmap

## Product goal

Develop the five-stop Bible activity collection into the second strong word-and-puzzle game in the catalog: approachable for children, varied from stop to stop, and tied together by a rewarding journey map.

## Phase 1 — Reliability and clarity

- Audit every activity for ambiguous answers, repeated-letter issues, small touch targets, and unclear success states.
- Standardize instructions, hint behavior, retry, pause, and progress saving across all five stops.
- Add a short playable example before the first puzzle type appears.
- Make every challenge completable without sound, color alone, or precision dragging.

## Phase 2 — Stronger puzzle design

- Give each story a signature mechanic: sequence, word path, matching, observation, and shepherding logic.
- Add multiple handcrafted boards per story with a smooth difficulty curve.
- Use hints that teach the next step rather than solving the entire puzzle.
- Add optional discovery challenges and collectable map stars.

## Phase 3 — Visual journey

- Upgrade the story map with animated paths, distinct landmarks, and visible completion rewards.
- Give every activity its own illustrated setting and consistent character art.
- Add tactile selection, correct-answer, mistake, and celebration animation.
- Improve the final screen so the completed journey feels earned.

## Phase 4 — Expansion

- Add a second map with five new Bible-story stops.
- Add replay modes with remixed puzzle layouts instead of faster timers.
- Add a family mode that reads instructions and story summaries aloud.

## Release gate

- Play every board with mouse, keyboard, and touch on portrait, landscape, tablet, and desktop.
- Verify accessibility, saves, reset, hints, read-aloud, and no soft locks.
- Refresh social preview, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio.
